Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 21, 2026 • Last Updated: September 2026 • Version: 1.0.6
🔒 Core Privacy Commitment: Snoozy is built upon a strict privacy-by-design architecture. We believe relaxation and sleep health should be private. Snoozy operates 100% locally on your device: no account registration, no background tracking, no microphones, and zero personal data collection.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Snoozy adheres to the highest data protection standards (including GDPR, CCPA, and Solana Mobile dApp Store Guidelines):

  • No Personal Data: We do not ask for, collect, store, or transmit your name, email address, phone number, physical location, or device identifiers.
  • No Audio Recording or Microphone Access: Snoozy is an acoustic synthesis and playback engine only. The app does not request or possess microphone permissions (`android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO`). It cannot and will not record your ambient audio or voice.
  • No Analytics or Behavioral Trackers: We do not embed invasive third-party telemetry, advertising SDKs (in the Solana edition), or analytics trackers (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Adjust, AppsFlyer).

2. On-Device Storage & Permissions

All app settings, sound volume levels, selected natural sound presets, and sleep timer configurations are stored exclusively on your local device using Android SharedPreferences:

  • Foreground Media Service (`android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK`): Used strictly to ensure audio continues playing smoothly while your screen is turned off or when you use other apps.
  • Wake Lock (`android.permission.WAKE_LOCK`): Used only during active sleep playback to prevent the Android OS from abruptly terminating the background audio engine.
  • Post Notifications (`android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS`): Used on Android 13+ solely to render the standard lock-screen media player notification (Play, Pause, Stop).

3. Web3 & Voluntary Support (Solana Edition)

In the Solana Mobile dApp Store edition, users may choose to support continuous development through voluntary tips:

  • Non-Custodial Architecture: Snoozy does not hold, manage, or have access to your private keys or seed phrases. Any donation interaction utilizes standard Solana Pay URIs (`solana:...`) or Mobile Wallet Adapter (MWA) protocol, handled entirely by your trusted device wallet (e.g., Solana Seeker Seed Vault or Phantom).
  • Public Blockchain Data: If you perform a blockchain donation, the transaction is publicly recorded on the decentralized Solana ledger as inherent to blockchain technology. Snoozy itself maintains no database associating your identity with wallet transactions.
  • Optional Coffee Tipping: For non-crypto voluntary support via Ko-fi or PayPal, donations are processed externally on respective secure platforms. Snoozy receives no payment details or card numbers.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Snoozy is an offline ambient white noise tool suitable for all ages, including babies, children, and parents. Because we do not collect any personal information whatsoever, Snoozy is fully compliant with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international standards.

5. Security of Your Data

Because your sound preferences and timer states never leave your physical device, your information is protected by your device's built-in sandbox security and hardware encryption.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update our Privacy Policy to reflect app updates or regulatory changes. Any modifications will be posted to this page with an updated effective date.
